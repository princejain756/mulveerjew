Company Story:
Mulveer Jewellers began its journey with a strong foundation in gold ornament manufacturing, specializing
in crafting fine gold jewellery. The brand later expanded into retail as the jewellery industry witnessed rapid
growth and evolving consumer demand.
The name “Mulveer” is derived from a revered form of Lord Shiva. Rooted in spiritual significance, the
brand embodies purity, trust, and craftsmanship. Mulveer Jewellers is dedicated to offering guaranteed,
authentic jewellery with a commitment to transparent pricing, minimal making charges, and designs that
balance tradition with modern aesthetics.
Vision Statement (drafted for your review):
To become a trusted household name in the jewellery industry by offering pure, certified, and beautifully
crafted ornaments that reflect timeless tradition and modern elegance.
Mission Statement (drafted for your review):
To consistently deliver jewellery of unmatched purity and design excellence; to nurture long-term trust
through honest pricing, personalized service, and innovation in every creation; and to make the divine
essence of “Mulveer” shine through every ornament we craft.

PRODUCTS & OFFERINGS
Primary Product Categories



Gold Jewellery — Traditional and contemporary ornaments crafted in 20K and 22K gold.



Silver Jewellery — Elegant silver articles and ornaments with guaranteed purity.



Diamond Jewellery — Certified and finely crafted diamond pieces for both daily wear and special
occasions.

Each collection blends artistic design, craftsmanship, and authenticity to deliver timeless elegance.

Custom / Made-to-Order Designs
Mulveer Jewellers specializes in custom jewellery services to bring each customer’s vision to life.
Customers can share or select a desired design, after which the team evaluates feasibility and coordinates
with the in-house workshop. Based on the chosen design, Mulveer provides an estimated weight, carat,
and cost before production.
This personalized approach ensures every piece reflects the customer’s individuality, with precision and
transparency at every step.
